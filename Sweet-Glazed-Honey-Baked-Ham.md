# Sweet Glazed Honey Baked Ham
This was phenomenal.  Link to the full recipe with detailed instructions is [in my bio](https://www.instagram.com/camcancoook_/)!

## Ingredients

### Ham:
- Bone-In Spiral Ham (Fully Cooked) - 10 Lbs
- Orange SlicesPineapple Slices (Canned)
- Lemon SlicesRosemaryOrange - 1 (For Squeezing)
- Honey Butter Marinade:Honey - 6 Oz
- Melted Butter - 8 Tbsp
- Orange Juice - 1 Large Orange
- Soy Sauce - 1 Tbsp
- Dijon Mustard - 1 1/2 Tbsp
- Pepper - 2 Tsp
- Garlic Powder - 1 Tbsp
- Smoked Paprika - 2 Tsp

### Sweet Citrus Glaze:
- Butter - 1 Stick
- Brown Sugar - 2/3 Cup
- White Sugar - 1/3 Cup
- Vanilla Bean Paste - 1 Tbsp
- Pineapple Juice - 12 Oz
- Light Or Dark Soy Sauce - 1 Tbsp
- Ginger Paste - 1 Tsp
- Cinnamon - 1/2 Tsp
- Orange Juice - 1 Orange
- Orange Zest - 1/2 Orange
- Lemon Juice - 1/2 Lemon
- Lemon Zest - 1/2 Lemon
- Cold Butter - 1/2 Stick

## Method

1. Whisk together the honey butter marinade ingredients.
2. Place the ham down and pour the marinade into every crevice.
3. Add the fruit slices and rosemary to a baking dish and squeeze in the orange juice. Place ham on top.
4. Bake at 350 for about 2 hours, basting every 30 minutes.
5. Melt the butter, then add the brown and white sugars, vanilla, pineapple juice, soy sauce, ginger, cinnamon and citrus.
6. Simmer for about 10 minutes, then stir in the cold butter off the heat.
7. Pour the glaze on and broil on high for 5 minutes.
8. Baste with the glaze and broil 2 more rounds of 5 minutes.
9. Rest for 15 minutes, then slice and enjoy!
