# Chef Zealand Mac & Cheese
Source: [Chef Zealand - Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/reels/Cz7Ie4oxLg_/)

## Ingredients

- 1/4 cup Butter (1 stick)
- 1/4 cup flour 
- 2 cups milk 
- 1 tsp Salt
- 1 tsp Fresh cracked pepper
- 1 tbsp garlic powder 
- 1 tsp cayenne pepper
- 1 tsp ground ginger
- 1 cup Sharp Cheddar (shredded)
- 1 cup Gruyère Cheese  (shredded)
- 1 cup Mozzarella Cheese (shredded)
- 1 cup Gouda Cheese (shredded)
- 1 boxes Macaroni Noodles 
- American Cheese (2 slices)
- Chives

## Method
Preheat your oven to 350F degrees before beginning these steps, to ensure it is ready when you're done.

1. Grate 1 cup each of the Sharp Cheddar, Gouda, Gruyère, and Mozzarella cheese
2. Melt a 1 8oz stick of butter into a pan or cast iron bowl on the stove
3. Sprinkle 1/4 cup of flower into the mixing bowl with the melted butter
4. Stir the flower and butter mixture for 2-3 minutes until fully mixed
5. Slowly add milk to the mixture while stirring until 2 cups have been fully added.
  - Mixture should be fairly thin when complete
6. Add in the garlic powder, cayenne pepper, ground ginger, salt, and pepper into the mixture
  - Stir until fully mixed
7. Add in your American Cheese slices and allow to melt
8. Add in the remainder of the shredded cheeses, stirring and allowing to melt fully
  - Continue to stir until mixture has thickened and is in a mostly-liquid consistency
9. Add in Macaroni noodles, stirring into mixture until noodles are fully covered
10. Pour into oven-safe baking dish
11. Strinkle extra shredded cheese onto top of mixture in oven-safe dish
12. Place dish into oven at 350F degrees for 20 minutes or until top layer of cheese has melted and slightly darkened.
13. Garnish the top of the baked dish with Chives.  Amount of chives is your call!

Allow the dish to cool, then serve!
